## Table S3. Library curation provenance note

On 2026-08-17, a network-failure rerun overwrote data/tcm/tcm_library_raw.csv (all resolution statuses lost) and tcm_library_filtered.csv (emptied). The surviving authoritative artifact is the CID-anchored 131-compound prediction table (np_fxr_predictions.csv, 21:09, all rows carrying PubChem CIDs), from which every downstream number (tiers, CW-BCS, representatives) recomputes exactly. On 2026-08-23 the pipeline (step4_tcm_library.py) was re-executed: resolution reproduced at 224, ADMET filter at 131, with **131/131 row-level CID concordance**. The 131 rows contain 129 unique CIDs because two rows share one CID—Gomisin K3 and Schisanhenol both resolve to CID 73057 (one of the two name resolutions at library-build time was likely erroneous; both are Schisandra lignans, the herb ranks 7th, and the impact is negligible) —and Formononetin legitimately appears once per source herb (CID 5280378). These are retained and flagged rather than silently deleted (results/v6/cid_concordance_diff.csv). No manuscript number depends on the overwritten intermediate. IL6 was absent from the DEG matrix (left blank in the target table; not used anywhere).
